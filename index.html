How SipSiren Works

  1. Pick the vessel & sound: Select glass, bottle, or tumbler (or dial in exact sipping seconds) and audition an annoying sound.
  2. Hit Start Watching: SipSiren opens the camera (all processing stays 100% on-device) and starts the alarm right away.
  3. Drink inside the Sip Zone: Raising the bottle/glass to her mouth inside the dashed box drains the water meter.
  4. No camera? No problem: Click Use Demo Cam (or hold the Hold to Sip button / Spacebar) to test the full experience anywhere.
